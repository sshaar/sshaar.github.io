A related thread to fact-checking: instead of asking *is this true?*, ask *how is this trying
to persuade you?* We built the **Prta** system for highlighting propaganda techniques in
news articles, and co-organized SemEval-2021 Task 6 on detecting persuasion techniques in
multimodal (text + image) memes.
