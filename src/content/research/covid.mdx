During the pandemic, the fact-checking problem collided with an information ecosystem
moving at unprecedented speed. We organized shared tasks and surveys cutting across
factuality, harmfulness, propaganda, and framing, in multiple languages, to build shared
evaluation resources for researchers and fact-checking organizations.
