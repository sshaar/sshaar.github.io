A collaboration with Cornell Medicine analyzing the narrative justifications doctors write
when requesting exceptions to the heart transplant allocation policy. Using LLMs for
thematic analysis, we surface recurring argument patterns across thousands of requests —
work with direct implications for allocation-policy design.
